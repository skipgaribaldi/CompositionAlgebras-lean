# Results, citation by citation

This is the detailed record of what the `CompositionAlgebras` library proves, indexed by the
citation in the source literature. It is the companion to [`README.md`](README.md), which gives
the narrative account of the same material layer by layer, and to [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md),
which says how the Lean is written.

Everything listed as proved is proved: the library builds with no `sorry` and no axioms beyond
`propext`, `Quot.sound` and `Classical.choice`. Rows that say **not done** record deliberate
boundaries — usually a missing mathlib prerequisite — and say what the missing input is.

Citations follow [`README.md`](README.md#references); `GPR`, `GN`, `EKM` and `Dias` are used
throughout without further introduction.

| `GPR 18.14(c)`, both directions | **proved** (`Conic/CayleyDicksonAlternative.lean`). `isAlternative_cay_iff`: `Cay(B, μ)` is alternative **iff** `B` is associative and its conjugation is an involution — for every scalar `μ`, with no hypothesis on `B` beyond the conic structure and nothing on `2`, the rank or the norm. Associativity is stated as the hypothesis `∀ a b c : B, a * b * c = a * (b * c)` rather than `[Ring B]`, because `⇒` produces it for a `B` that is only a `NonAssocRing` and cannot produce an instance; `isAlternative_cay` is the `[Ring B] [Algebra k B]` restatement the tower consumes. The `⇒` direction is what stops the tower at rank 8, and its two halves are each one component of one alternative law: `conjIsInvolution_of_isAlternative_cay` is the `j`-part of left alternativity at `x = a + j`, `y = c` (taking the `j`-part of `x` to be `1` is what makes the trace defect visible with no faithfulness hypothesis — `GPR 16.10(a)` only places it in `Ann(B)`, but `B` is unital), and `mul_assoc_of_isAlternative_cay` is the `j`-part of the **linearized** right alternative law at `x = a`, `y = b`, `z = cj`. | Multiplicative conic algebras and norm-associativity (`GPR 19.1`, `GPR 16.5`) | **proved** (`Conic/Multiplicative.lean`). `ConicAlgebra.IsMultiplicative` is `n(xy) = n(x)n(y)`, a predicate on the conic structure and not a bundle. | Kirmse's identities and `GPR 19.3` | **proved in full** (`Conic/Kirmse.lean`, `Conic/Recognition.lean`). `Conic/Recognition.lean` derives the conic structure: for a unital algebra with a quadratic form `n` that is non-degenerate (`radical n = 0`), normalized and multiplicative, the linearizations `GPR (19.1.3)`–`(19.1.5)` give `n(w, ·) = 0` and `n(w) = 0` for `w = x² − t(x)x + n(x)·1`, so `ConicAlgebra.ofMultiplicative` is a conic algebra with norm `n`, with no module hypothesis; `eq_ofMultiplicative` makes `n` the unique conic norm on a finite projective algebra; the polar radical `ker n.polarBilin` is a two-sided ideal (`IsMultiplicative.polarRadical`) in the center (`mem_center_of_mem_ker_polarBilin`), killed by `2` and by every `n(x, y)` (`polar_smul_eq_zero_of_mem_ker_polarBilin`). The **Kirmse defect** `w = x̄(xy) − n(x)·y` lies in `QuadraticMap.radical` of the norm in *every* multiplicative conic algebra, with no hypothesis whatsoever (`mem_radical_conj_mul_mul`, `mem_radical_mul_mul_conj`): `n(w, z) = 0` is two applications of norm-associativity, and `n(w) = 0` is the cancellation `n(x)²n(y) + n(x)²n(y) − n(x)²·n(y, y) = 0` using `n(y, y) = 2n(y)`. So the hypothesis that cancels `w` is exactly **`GPR`'s non-degeneracy** `radical n = 0` (`QuadraticForm.IsGPRNondegenerate`), which is what `GPR 19.3` assumes — *not* injectivity of the polar form. Kirmse's identities `x̄(xy) = n(x)y`, `(yx)x̄ = n(x)y` follow, and substituting `x̄ = t(x)·1 − x` turns them into the two alternative laws (`IsMultiplicative.isAlternative`) — no assumption on `2`, on the rank, or on the module. ⚠ Over a **ring**, non-singular does **not** imply non-degenerate (`⟨1⟩` over `𝔽₂[t]/(t²)` is non-singular with `t` in its radical), so `IsComposition ⇒ IsAlternative` (`Composition/Basic.lean`) is proved **over a field** with no hypothesis on `2` (`IsComposition.isAlternative`, via `radical_eq_bot_of_radical_baseChangeOfProjective_eq_bot`) and **over a ring with `2 ∈ kˣ`** (`isAlternative_of_invertible_two`, via `GN (C.8.2)`). ⚠ The general ring case is **now closed, but not by a descent** (`Composition/Alternative.lean`). The descent this README used to record is wrong: residue fields cannot detect a nilpotent. Multiplication by `t` on `𝔽₂[t]/(t²)` is a nonzero map of finitely generated free modules that vanishes after base change to the one residue field — indeed to every field over that ring — so "a map of f.g. modules vanishing at every residue field is zero" is false, and it was the load-bearing step.
